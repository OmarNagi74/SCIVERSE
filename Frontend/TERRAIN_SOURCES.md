# Terrain sources and bounded frontend proof

## Chosen lunar source

The local proof uses **SLDEM2015_512_60S_30S_315_360**, product version V2.0, PDS dataset `LRO-L-LOLA-4-GDR-V1.0`. Official numeric JP2: https://imbrium.mit.edu/EXTRAS/SLDEM2015/TILES/JP2/SLDEM2015_512_60S_30S_315_360.JP2 . Its PDS3 label is https://imbrium.mit.edu/EXTRAS/SLDEM2015/TILES/JP2/SLDEM2015_512_60S_30S_315_360_JP2.LBL and auxiliary raster metadata is https://imbrium.mit.edu/EXTRAS/SLDEM2015/TILES/JP2/SLDEM2015_512_60S_30S_315_360_AUX.XML . The official file is 169,985,544 bytes. Its observed SHA-256 is recorded in the generated local manifest, not duplicated in this note.

The tile is 23,040×15,360 at 512 pixels per degree; simple cylindrical, planetocentric; 315°E–360°E and 60°S–30°S; body-fixed MEAN EARTH/POLAR AXIS OF DE421. It is merged LOLA / SELENE terrain-camera elevation referenced to the 1,737,400 m lunar radius. NASA/PDS documents the height formula as `HEIGHT = (DATA VALUE × 0.5) + 1,737,400 m`.[1] The JP2 decoder used here exposes an unsigned 16-bit component. Removing its 32,768 sample bias reproduces the official AUX actual range: raw 20,546 → −12,222 and raw 37,621 → 4,853. This independently confirms the sample transformation against the source range. The AUX reports 0.5 scale and decoded `−32768` no-data; therefore raw JP2 value 0 decodes to `−32768` no-data. Other decoded counts convert to relative relief as `count × 0.5 m`. The 1,737,400 m offset is provenance/reference context and is not added to the local mesh’s relative vertex heights.

The derived output is a 129×129, signed little-endian grid covering the Tycho-area bounds 40°–46°S and 15°–7°W (345°–353°E). The script keeps elevation samples separate from the existing GLD100 color imagery, maps their geography through independent UV coordinates, and records source hash, crop pixel bounds, source mode, offset, scale, no-data, sample extrema and counts, derived hash, and display limits in `client/public/assets/terrain/tycho-sldem2015.json`. The source file is stored only under ignored `assets/source/` and is excluded from Git/build output; only compact derived static assets ship. Terrain mode is available only when Tycho is selected. It supplies no scientific elevation readout or site-specific finding; exaggeration changes display only. No-data cells do not receive triangles. Neither the local patch nor GLD100 texture covers the polar Shackleton reference. The texture-derived preview remains strictly visual; no imagery pixel is used as a height.

Source and product documentation: NASA GSFC SLDEM2015 product overview https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/54 ; PDS Geosciences product help https://ode.rsl.wustl.edu/moon/pagehelp/Content/Missions_Instruments/LRO/LOLA/SLDEM.htm ; USGS merged DEM catalog https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/moon_lro_lola_selene_kaguya_tc_dem_merge_60n60s_59m ; archive SIS https://imbrium.mit.edu/DOCUMENT/archsis.htm . ## Other lunar products evaluated

USGS GLD100: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/moon_lroc_wac_dtm_gld100_118m . The true DTM covers 79°S–79°N and is ~10 GB; the bundled image is a separate visual preview only. USGS LOLA global DEM: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/moon_lro_lola_dem_118m . The full product is ~8 GB, 256 ppd, signed 16-bit with 0.5 scale, referenced to a 1,737.4 km sphere, and includes interpolation gaps. USGS South Pole NAC DEM: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/moon_lro_south_pole_dem . This 4 m/pixel polar stereographic product is a limited stereo region (~310 MB), not a global South Pole raster; it was not selected because the host download was unavailable and exact overlap with current reference coverage was not established.

## Mars options evaluated, not activated

USGS MOLA DEM: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_mgs_mola_dem_463m . The 128 ppd / ~463 m-at-equator product is ~2 GB, CC0, measured relative to the GMM-2B areoid with interpolated gaps and sparse polar observations. USGS HRSC/MOLA blend v2: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_mgs_mola_mex_hrsc_blended_dem_global_200m . This ~11 GB global 200 m product blends CC0 MOLA with HRSC under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO; the source notes blend seams and ~44% HRSC coverage in this release. Both remain later candidates only. Mars stays color-visualization-only in this phase.
